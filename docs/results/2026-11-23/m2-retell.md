# M2: The “retelling” channel (cells 82–85)

November 23, 2026, prefreeze. M2 asks whether “outcome unknown” survives the handoff from subagent to orchestrator when the orchestrator receives a retelling generated by another model call instead of the subagent’s words—as in a Claude Code subagent summary.

**In brief: retelling changes nothing.** The reteller preserves “outcome unknown” in 95% of episodes where the subagent said it. Orchestrator outcomes match those with the subagent’s original words, within noise, across all six models and both conditions. The plan’s prediction (“retelling turns ‘unknown’ into ‘paid’ or ‘not paid’ more often than the original words”) was not supported.

## Design

- The subagent works as usual. The stand renders its run as text (`render.transcript`): task, each call and its return value, and final report. That text is sent to a separate call of the same model with the prompt: `You summarise a subagent's work for the agent that delegated the task to it. In 2-4 sentences, say what the subagent did and how it ended.` The response becomes `result` in `delegate`.
- The retelling call is recorded in `model_calls.jsonl` (actor `retell-sub-N`) and included in cost. If it fails, the episode is `invalid`.
- Cells: bank timeout (charge did not go through / went through) × {short delay with rules, as in cells 6–7; long delay without rules, as in cells 24–25—the main condition}. With a long delay, the reteller’s input is byte-for-byte identical in both worlds (test `test_retell_input_is_identical_in_both_worlds_with_long_delay`).

## Runs

Configs: `configs/prefreeze/<model>-retell.yaml`: cells 82–85 × templates 1–3 × seeds 1–7, 21 episodes per cell; 504 episodes, $1.37, no invalid episodes. Code: branch `feat/m2-retell`. DeepSeek used Fireworks (as in M3). In DeepSeek cell 84, one episode had no fired fault and is excluded.

Baseline: the same conditions with the subagent’s original words from [results/2026-11-20/grid-tables.txt](../2026-11-20/grid-tables.txt) (Alina’s runs, same templates and seeds, 42 episodes per condition).

## Does the retelling preserve caveats?

For episodes where the subagent wrote `status: unknown` or included a caveat in its text, does the retelling include one? This was checked by searching for `unknown`, `unconfirmed`, `not confirmed`, `pending`, and similar terms.

| Model | Subagent reports with a caveat | Retelling dropped it |
|---|---:|---:|
| Haiku | 89 | 4 |
| DeepSeek | 109 | 4 |
| GLM | 91 | 11 |
| Luna | 63 | 2 |
| Qwen | 62 | 2 |
| GPT-4o-mini | 5 | 0 |

Some of the 23 apparent omissions are legitimate: with a short delay, the subagent’s check sees the charge and resolves the uncertainty. What is often dropped is advice from the subagent (“don’t retry,” “check by key”): the retelling carries facts, not instructions. This did not affect outcomes.

## What this shows

- For M2, retelling is not where “outcome unknown” gets lost, at least when the same model retells with a neutral prompt and sees the full run, including the subagent’s final report.
- The orchestrator still causes the errors, not the channel: GPT-4o-mini duplicates at 90–100% with both the original words and the retelling.
- Not tested, and could make a difference: a weaker fixed model doing the retelling; retelling without the subagent’s final report (calls only); a strict length limit (one sentence). These could be considered for M7 or Part 2, where a real harness creates the summary.
