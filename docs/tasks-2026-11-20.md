November 20, 2026. Part 1: run on one computer per person, without real harnesses. All runs are **prefreeze**: identify where models fail and what protects them before freezing the benchmark and preregistering. Earlier runs are described in [results/2026-11-19/](results/2026-11-19/README.md).

## 1. Current status

- **The boundary is risky only when verification is unavailable.** If the orchestrator can inspect the bank itself or through a new subagent, it can learn the truth without relying on the report, and there are almost no errors. If the charge is hidden (late commit), all models make errors.
- **The boundary effect varies by model.** Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna, GLM-5.3-Flash, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash check and avoid errors when verification is possible. Qwen 3.7 Flash and GPT-4o-mini trust the report and say “paid” when nothing was charged in 86–90% of cases; duplicate rates are 8–50%.
- **Calibration without the loophole:** models that check make duplicates (Haiku 43%, Luna 86%, GLM 76%, DeepSeek 86% in post with words); trusting models falsely report “paid” in pre. The statement removes duplicates for Haiku and Luna, and partly for GLM and DeepSeek. Figures here and below use the corrected hiding rule (DECISIONS, 2026-11-21; [results/2026-11-20/](results/2026-11-20/README.md)).
- **M4, idempotency keys** (main hypothesis). Duplicate rates when verification is impossible:

  | Key issuer | Haiku | DeepSeek | GLM | GPT-4o-mini | Luna | Qwen |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | K0 — none | 95% | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% |
  | K1 — subagent | 57% | 83% | 90% | 100% (does not pass on key) | 90% | 100% |
  | K2 — orchestrator includes it in task | 0% | 2% | 0% | 19% | 0% | 7% |
  | K3 — system derives it from task number | 36% | 62% | 64% | 50% | 40% | 33% |
  | K4 — system derives it from payment intent | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** |
  | K5 — system pins the first request | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** |

  When verification is possible, there are no duplicates with any key. Models invent meaningful keys (`INV-104-cus_ann-86200`) but do not repeat the exact string, which the bank requires. K3 fails on a new task (new number, new key) and blocks a legitimate second payment in one task. There are 42 episodes per key across respawn and redelivery, `*-keys-hidden-v3`.
- **Key transfer from a statement.** When the statement exposes the key (cells 60–63), the orchestrator sometimes passes it on when delegating the same invoice again: DeepSeek in 22 of 34 episodes, GLM in 21 of 37, and Haiku in 7 of 27 (0 when the statement does not show the key; `scripts/focal_key.py`). Duplicates fall for GLM in the world where the charge succeeded, from 71% to 38%, and for DeepSeek from 38% to 5%.
- **The grader** matched manual labels for stronger models; with weaker models it can err when `finish` fields conflict with the text (24 of 30 across the full sample): [grader-check](results/2026-11-19/grader-check.md).

## 2. Before starting (everyone)

1. Run `git pull` and `uv sync`. PR #12 (harder world) and the M4 branch (keys, statement with key, scripts) need to be available.
2. Put your own key in `.env` (see `.env.example`) and set a spending limit. Budget $40–50 for Dima running Opus and $10 for everyone else.
3. Warm up with `uv run stand batch configs/prefreeze/01-smoke-haiku.yaml` (11 episodes, $0.02), then run the condition closest to your hypothesis (listed below). Compare figures with [results/2026-11-19/](results/2026-11-19/README.md) and inspect 3–4 episodes: `uv run python scripts/show.py runs/<run>/<episode>`.
4. Store raw logs in `runs/` (not in Git; share via Drive), reports in `docs/results/<date>/`, decisions in `DECISIONS.md`, and configs in `configs/`. Name runs `prefreeze-…`.
