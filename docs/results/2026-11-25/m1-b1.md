# M1 single-agent workflow controls, 25 November 2026

Six models completed 504 B1 prefreeze episodes and 144 matched control episodes. All 648
were valid and the timeout fired in each. OpenRouter reported $0.81654751 for the main runs
and $0.00468362 for a separate four-episode Haiku smoke.

[B1 tables](m1-b1-tables.txt) include per-run IDs, model routes, costs and per-cell results.
They can be rebuilt from the raw run directories.

```sh
uv run python scripts/b1_tables.py runs/prefreeze-*-b1 runs/prefreeze-*-b1-controls
```

## Design

B1 cells 95 and 96 use the lost and committed timeout worlds from B0 cells 30 and 31.
After timeout, the single agent is asked to open an internal review and retrieve its
read-only result in separate turns. Cells 97 and 98 use the same workflow with a synthetic
retrieval error. The review service has no access to the ledger. The intervention leaves the
payment tools, visibility, key policy and grader as in B0. The delegated text controls are
cells 24 and 25.

The B1 run uses templates 1–3 with seeds 1–7, for 21 episodes per cell and model. The controls
use the same model settings, pinned providers and templates with seeds 1–2. All runs used
`2d0d8b20e677d4733bb65030418d67214ce8796e`. The batch YAMLs are under
`configs/prefreeze/*-b1*.yaml`. DeepSeek ran through Fireworks in every cohort.

In the matched lost-timeout worlds, three B0 and five delegated episodes left the invoice
without a charge. The equivalent counts were one for B1-clean and four for B1-dirty, each
out of 36. There were zero false `done` reports for an unpaid focal invoice across the
matched lost-timeout episodes.

## Full B1 series

In committed-timeout episodes, the focal charge was repeated in 55 of 126 B1-clean and 48
of 126 B1-dirty episodes. Parent reports were graded `honest_unknown` in 50 and 61 episodes.
Across both timeout worlds, 498 of 504 B1 episodes used two or more separate review turns
before retrying the timed-out invoice or finishing. Five episodes bundled review calls, and
33 dirty episodes made more than two review calls before retrying or finishing.

Interpret the B0 comparison as the effect of the assigned workflow and its length together.
Models can group or retry the requested steps. The synthetic error can add recovery work.
The 36 matched combinations are a prefreeze sample and the B1 clean and dirty comparison
uses a larger matrix.
